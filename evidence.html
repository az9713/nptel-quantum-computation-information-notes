Evidence, scope, and limitations

The source corpus contains 60 recordings. English auto-captions cover 38; local faster-whisper transcripts supply the remaining 22 after caption requests were blocked. Machine recognition can garble mathematical words and numbers. Authors compare important expressions with derivations, primary references, and selected frames.

Selected-frame inspection is recorded in chapter sidecars. A complete audiovisual review, formal proof verification, experimental apparatus reproduction, and human accessibility certification are not claimed.

Chapter drafts, independent reviews, repaired content, numerical execution, static links, and rendered-reader behavior are different evidence dimensions. Review reports are retained privately; curated verdict metadata is provided here.
