Quantum information, from states to open systems

A mathematically developed study edition of all 60 source recordings: qubits and interference, information and entanglement, communication and factoring, noise and measurement. Definitions, derivations, runnable examples, and solved exercises share one notation contract.

20 chapters completed; 20 independently accepted · 120 worked exercises · 20 chapter laboratories.

The course is taught by Prof. Chiranjib Mitra at IISER Kolkata and coordinated by IIT Madras. Official course. The official teaching order is restored from the shuffled YouTube playlist.

The available captions and local machine transcripts were used as source evidence. The edition contains independently authored derivations and examples. Topic coverage, selected visual checks, scientific review, numerical execution, and browser tests are separate claims; consult the evidence record for their actual status.

  16. 16. Noise channels: flips, depolarization, and damping

    Distinguish coherence loss, population relaxation, and parameter conventions.

  17. 17. Master equations and relaxation times

    Connect a stated Markovian generator to populations and coherence.

  18. 18. Generalized measurements and POVMs

    Separate measurement probabilities from post-measurement dynamics.

  19. 19. Mixed-state entanglement, convexity, and partial transpose

    Distinguish classical mixing from inseparability and state dimension limits.
